Your escape plan

Getting out of debt is possible. Here's how.

Step by step strategies for people who are done letting debt control their life. Free, practical, and built for real situations.

Your 5 Step Debt Escape Plan

01

Know Your Numbers

List every debt you have — balance, interest rate, minimum payment. You can't fight what you don't face. This step alone reduces anxiety.

02

Stop the Bleeding

Cut any spending that isn't essential and stop adding to your debt immediately. No new credit card charges during the payoff period.

03

Build a Tiny Emergency Fund

Save $500–$1,000 first before attacking debt hard. This prevents one unexpected expense from derailing everything.

04

Choose Your Payoff Method

Snowball (smallest balance first) for motivation. Avalanche (highest interest first) for maximum savings. Both work — pick one and commit.

05

Find Extra Income

Even an extra $100–$200 a month accelerates debt payoff dramatically. Side hustles, selling items, or picking up extra hours all count.
